Heroes Make Powerful Lessons

Hero gives you more powers to supercharge your classrooms readings. Engage your students with questions, in reading discussion, and more!

As a Hero, you get:

Reading Questions
Gather more insight into your students thoughts as they read. Ask them what they learned or how their understanding of the topic has changed. Choose if they can answer with emoji, text, or by recording their own audio answer!

Comment Replies
Replies let you or your readers start discussion right in the text. Reach out to a student and ask them to expand on their comment, or let students talk to eachother to explore a topic even more in depth on their own!

Custom Group Codes
Share a reading with a custom code you select to be memorable and easier for your students to reach. Codes can describe the reading, an answer to a trivia question, or a fun to get students even more excited about reading!

Audio Comments (BETA)
Let your students record an Audio Comment as they read so they can put their thoughts directly into the reading for their classmates to hear. Great for students that struggle with writing, or students that are so excited they just cant wait to write their thoughts down!


(BETA) Audio comments are currently only available on Windows computers and Chromebooks while using the Google Chrome Browser. iPad and MacBook functionality is coming soon!
